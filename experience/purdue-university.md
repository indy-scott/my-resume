# Purdue University Computing Center — Assistant Network Systems Engineer

## Responsibilities and Accomplishments

- Helped install Cisco network switches and routers supporting Purdue University's campus network.
- Installed and activated network connections to provide service to labeled physical ports.
- Focused primarily on physical installation and cabling rather than switch or router configuration.
- Assisted with connectivity troubleshooting and verified that newly installed network devices and attached systems operated correctly.
- Developed practical knowledge of TCP/IP, routing, and network fundamentals and applied it during installation verification and troubleshooting.
- Installed or changed dozens of network connections on a typical workday, with large installations reaching approximately 50 connections in one day.
- Helped install two to three Cisco switches and one to two routers in a rack during large deployments.
- Helped install and maintain six Windows computer labs and one Solaris workstation lab, ranging from roughly 12 to nearly 50 workstations per lab.
- Performed end-to-end physical workstation deployment: unpacking, assembly, network connection, initial boot, software initialization, and campus-network login testing for final sign-off.
- Deployed network printers in computer labs and replaced aging printer hardware.
- Performed physical installation and maintenance in the primary data center, which housed the Cisco campus-network backbone and servers supporting network operations.
- Installed and replaced cards, ports, and cabling as data-center equipment was deployed, moved, or maintained.
- Maintained a manager-designed spreadsheet inventory of contractor-installed network drops and physical connectivity.
- Recorded switch-to-patch-panel port mappings, cable lengths, inter-switch and router connections, and whether each connection used a crossover cable.
- Used the detailed records to determine the required quantities and lengths of cables before traveling to a network closet, improving preparation for installation and maintenance work.

## Technologies and Practices

Cisco switches and routers, TCP/IP, routing fundamentals, Windows PCs, Solaris workstations, campus networking, data-center networking, physical network ports and drops, cross-connect and crossover cabling, equipment racks, workstation deployment, network printers, hardware validation, spreadsheet-based infrastructure documentation.
